How it works

Four steps, about five minutes. A parent always goes first.

  1. Parent signs up

    Create your parent account on your phone. Add a second parent or guardian if you like.

  2. Add your kid

    You make your kid's account and pick their sign-in PIN. You agree to the rules for them.

  3. Friends + approvals

    Kids swap a private friend code. Both kids' parents approve before they can chat, play together or make plans.

  4. Hang, play, learn!

    Check-ins, rides, games, Future Me — with parents notified for the important stuff.

Hang out — safely

Check in / check out

Know whose house they're at

Kids check in when they get to a friend's house — like checking into a hotel. The host kid or host parent can check the whole group in and out, too.

  • You get an alert the moment your kid checks in or out
  • Host families see everyone who's at their house
  • Only at your own house or an approved friend's house
Pickup heads-up + I'm Ready

"I'm 10 minutes away"

Tap Heads-up on your way and your kid (and the host family) get a big alert to start packing up. Your kid can tap I'm Ready! to tell you they're ready to go.

  • Loud alert sounds plus a pop-up they can't miss
  • They tap "Got it" so you know they saw it
Rain + snow rides

Pouring at 3:50? Ask for a ride.

Let's Hang checks the weather for the walk to and from school. When rain, snow, storms or freezing cold are coming, kids see an "Ask for a ride" button — for themselves and their friends.

  • Every rider's parents get the request at once
  • First parent to tap "I've got it!" drives — everyone else is told
Plans + parent messages

Every parent confirms the plan

Kids (or parents) make a plan — pizza and a movie on Friday, the skate park on Saturday. Each kid's parent taps ✓ Confirm, and the plan only goes on the calendar once everyone has. Message the other parents right from the plan.

  • See exactly who's confirmed: "✓ Sam: confirmed by Kim"
  • The school calendar is built in — breaks and days off are marked
📻

Talk — approved friends only

Chat + walkie-talkie

Push-to-talk with real friends

One-to-one chat with friends both parents approved — plus a walkie-talkie: hold the 📻 button, talk, let go to send. Friends hear a beep and it plays right away.

  • Up to 15 seconds per clip — fun, not endless
  • Parents can listen to every clip and read every message
  • Clips delete themselves after 30 days
Code names

Agent Taco, reporting for duty 🕵️

Friends can give each other secret code names like "Major Unicorn" — it shows up in their friend list and chats. Just for fun between friends.

  • Code names go through the same bad-word filter as chat
  • Parents always see real names

Who's on top?

  • No public rankings — strangers never see your kid's scores

Play a friend

Sea Battle + Word Duel

Challenge an approved friend to Sea Battle (take turns hunting each other's hidden ships) or Word Duel (pick a secret word for each other — fewest misses wins). The app pings you when it's your turn.

  • Approved friends only — same rule as chat
  • Secret words come from a built-in kid-safe list

Learn — Future Me

Short, middle-school-friendly lessons with quick quizzes, badges and tools they can actually play with. Kids go at their own pace.

Career Quiz + Career Path

What could the future you be?

A 36-question career interest inventory gives kids their interest code — like Thinker · Doer · Creator — with careers to explore, the training each one needs, classes they might love and clubs to try now.

  • 5 career-planning lessons: paths after high school (college, trade school, apprenticeships, military), skills employers want, SMART goals
  • My Career Plan they can fill in and print
Money Smarts + Loan Lab

How money really works

10 lessons: earning and taxes, needs vs. wants, budgeting, saving and compound interest, banks, debit vs. credit, credit scores, smart shopping, scams — and finance charges on cars, homes and student loans.

  • Loan Lab: slide the price, down payment, rate and years — see the monthly payment and total interest (15 vs. 30-year mortgage!)
  • Budget Builder, Savings Goal, Money Grower, Credit Card Cost, Needs vs. Wants game
Stock Market + Stock Challenge

The real stock market — with play money

7 lessons on supply and demand, free enterprise and capitalism, what a stock is, why prices move, indexes, bull and bear markets, dividends and diversifying. Then kids get $10,000 of play money to trade real companies at real prices — Apple, Roblox, Disney, Nike, McDonald's and more, plus an S&P 500 index fund.

  • Live ticker · trades only while the real market is open
  • Compete with approved friends on a portfolio leaderboard
  • It's pretend money — nothing real is bought or sold

Questions

Does my kid need their own phone?
It helps, but it isn't required. Let's Hang works in the web browser on any phone, tablet or computer.
Do I need to download an app?
No. Just go to letshang.cool. On iPhone you can add it to your Home Screen so it opens like an app — the guide shows how.
Can kids sign up by themselves?
No. Only a parent or guardian can create a kid's account, and they agree to the rules when they do.
Who can message, walkie-talkie or play with my kid?
Only friends that both you and the other kid's parent approved. There's no search, no public profiles, and no way for strangers to find your kid.
Can I hear the walkie-talkie messages?
Yes. Open Home → your kid → Friends & safety and open a chat — voice clips have a ▶️ play button, just like your kid sees. Clips are deleted after 30 days.
Is the stock market real money?
No. Every kid gets $10,000 of play money. The stock prices are real (from the U.S. market, slightly delayed), so it feels real — but nothing is actually bought or sold, and no money ever changes hands.
Do the games cost anything or have ads?
No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no loot boxes anywhere in Let's Hang.
Is the career quiz a test?
No — it's an interest inventory about what your kid enjoys. There are no wrong answers, and results are meant to spark conversations. Their plan and results are saved on their own device.
Who can see my kid's game scores?
Only your kid and their approved friends. Leaderboards are never public.
Will I get text messages?
Only if you turn them on. You can get texts for approvals, check-ins, pickups, ride requests and "I'm Ready!" — reply STOP any time.
Why don't I hear alert sounds?
Sounds play while Let's Hang is open. Make sure your iPhone's side ringer switch is on and the volume is up, and tap the screen once after opening the app. A big pop-up shows for every alert even on silent.
Can two parents share one family?
Yes. Add a second parent or guardian from Account → Family. Each gets their own sign-in and alerts.

Ready to hang?

Set up your family in about five minutes. Then hand your kid their username and PIN — that's it.
