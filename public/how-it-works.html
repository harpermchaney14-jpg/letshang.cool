Let's Hang is the app for after-school hangouts. Kids check in at a friend's house, make plans, chat with approved friends and ask for a ride when it's pouring. Parents get a heads-up for every bit of it — and nothing happens without a parent's OK.

How it works

Four steps, about five minutes. A parent always goes first.

  1. Parent signs up

    Create your parent account on your phone. Add a second parent or guardian if you like.

  2. Add your kid

    You make your kid's account and pick their sign-in PIN. You agree to the rules for them.

  3. Friends + approvals

    Kids swap a private friend code. Both kids' parents approve before they can chat or make plans.

  4. Hang out!

    Check in at a friend's house, make plans, ask for rides — parents get notified every step.

What it does

Check in / check out

Know whose house they're at

Kids check in when they get to a friend's house — like checking into a hotel. The host kid or host parent can check the whole group in and out, too.

  • You get an alert the moment your kid checks in or out
  • Host families see everyone who's at their house
  • Only at your own house or an approved friend's house
Pickup heads-up + I'm Ready

"I'm 10 minutes away"

Tap Heads-up on your way and your kid (and the host family) get a big alert to start packing up. Your kid can tap I'm Ready! to tell you they're ready to go.

  • Loud alert sounds plus a pop-up they can't miss
  • They tap "Got it" so you know they saw it
Rain + snow rides

Pouring at 3:50? Ask for a ride.

Let's Hang checks the weather for the walk to and from school. When rain, snow, storms or freezing cold are coming, kids see an "Ask for a ride" button — for themselves and their friends.

  • Every rider's parents get the request at once
  • First parent to tap "I've got it!" drives — everyone else is told
  • Can't make it after all? Tap once and the others are asked again
Plans

Every parent confirms the plan

Kids (or parents) make a plan — pizza and a movie on Friday, the skate park on Saturday. Each kid's parent taps ✓ Confirm, and the plan only goes on the calendar once everyone has.

  • See exactly who's confirmed: "✓ Sam: confirmed by Kim"
  • Message the other parents right from the plan
Parent messages

Talk to the other parents

Message your co-parent and the parents of your kid's approved friends — to sort out drop-offs, pickups and sleepovers without hunting for phone numbers.

School calendar + walk weather

Knows when there's no school

The 2026–27 Sioux Falls school calendar is built in — breaks, holidays and inservice days are marked, and the walk times skip days off. Each morning and afternoon shows the walk weather and what to wear.

Chat

Chat — approved friends only

One-to-one chat with friends both parents have approved. Strangers can't find or message your kid. You can read your kid's chats any time.

Questions

Does my kid need their own phone?
It helps, but it isn't required. Let's Hang works in the web browser on any phone, tablet or computer — kids can sign in on a family iPad, a friend's house computer, or their own phone.
Do I need to download an app?
No. Just go to letshang.cool. On iPhone you can add it to your Home Screen so it opens like an app — the guide shows how.
Can kids sign up by themselves?
No. Only a parent or guardian can create a kid's account, and they agree to the rules when they do.
Who can message my kid?
Only friends that both you and the other kid's parent approved. There's no search, no public profiles, and no way for strangers to find your kid.
Who can message me?
Your co-parent, and the parents of your kid's approved friends. Kids can't use parent messages.
Will I get text messages?
Only if you turn them on. You can get texts for approvals, check-ins, pickups, ride requests and "I'm Ready!" — reply STOP any time. Everything also shows in the app with an alert sound.
Why don't I hear alert sounds?
Sounds play while Let's Hang is open. Make sure your iPhone's side ringer switch is on and the volume is up, and tap the screen once after opening the app. A big pop-up shows for every alert even on silent.
Can two parents share one family?
Yes. Add a second parent or guardian from Account → Family. Each gets their own sign-in and alerts.

Ready to hang?

Set up your family in about five minutes. Then hand your kid their username and PIN — that's it.
