1 · Parent setup

Parent
1

Create your parent account

  1. On your phone, go to letshang.cool.
  2. Tap I'M A PARENT, then Create a parent account.
  3. Enter your name, a username and a password (8+ characters).
  4. Add your mobile number and tick the box if you want text alerts (optional — you can do it later).
  5. Tap CREATE ACCOUNT.

📝 Save your recovery code. Right after signing up you'll see a code like 7KQ2-M48X-PL3D. Screenshot it or write it down — it's the only way to reset your password.

2

Add your kid

  1. On your home screen tap Add your kid (or Account → Family → + Kid).
  2. Pick an avatar — kids never upload photos.
  3. Enter a display name (first name or nickname), a username and a PIN (4+ characters).
  4. Tick the box confirming you're their parent or guardian and agree to the rules.
  5. Tap CREATE ACCOUNT.

👉 Give your kid their username and PIN. Have more than one kid? Repeat this step for each.

3

Add a second parent (optional)

  1. Go to Account → Family and tap + Add another parent / guardian.
  2. Enter their name, a username, a password and their cell phone.
  3. Send them their username and password (and their recovery code).

Already added someone without a number? Tap + Add cell next to their name.

4

Add your hang house

  1. Go to Account → Our hang house and tap + ADD.
  2. Give it a name like "The Lee House". The address is optional.
  3. Add house rules if you like — "Shoes off, homework first!"

Your house shows up for your kids and for your kids' approved friends, so they can check in when they come over.

5

Check the school day

Under Account → School day tap Edit. It's already set for Sioux Falls: be there 8:40, bell 9:00, out 3:50, and a 1-mile walk (about 20 min). Change anything that's different for your family.

The 2026–27 Sioux Falls school calendar is built in, so breaks and days off are already marked.

2 · Your kid signs in

Kid
6

First sign-in

  1. On their phone (or a family tablet), go to letshang.cool.
  2. Tap I'M A KID and enter the username and PIN you made.
  3. They pick their style — Neon or Pastel — and an avatar.
  4. They read the safety rules and tap to agree.

Forgot the PIN? You can change it: Home → your kid → Friends & safety → Name / avatar / PIN.

3 · Friends

Kid + parents
7

Swap friend codes

  1. Your kid opens the Friends tab. Their private code looks like HANG-M48X.
  2. They share it in person with a friend they know in real life (or tap Share).
  3. The friend types the code under Add a friend and taps Send friend request.
  4. Your kid accepts the request.

🔒 Codes should only be shared with real-life friends. Your kid can get a new code any time (Profile → New code) so old copies stop working.

8

Both parents approve

  1. You get an alert like "Approve Maya + Sam?"
  2. Open Approvals and tap APPROVE (or Decline).
  3. The other kid's parent does the same on their phone.

Once both parents approve, the kids can chat, make plans, check in at each other's houses and share rides. You can also message the other parent from 💬 Parents.

Plans work the same way: each kid's parent taps ✓ Confirm before a plan is on the calendar.

4 · Alerts + sounds

Everyone
9

Turn the sound on

  1. Make sure the ringer switch on the side of the iPhone is on (no orange showing) and the volume is up.
  2. Open Settings (kids) or Account (parents) → 🔊 Sounds.
  3. Tap a TEST button — you'll hear the sound and see the pop-up.

iPhones only allow sound after you tap the screen once, so tap anywhere after opening the app. Sounds play while Let's Hang is open; a big pop-up shows even on silent. For alerts when the phone is locked, turn on text alerts in Account.

5 · Put it on your Home Screen

Everyone
10

Open it like an app

iPhone — Safari

  1. Open letshang.cool in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

iPhone — Chrome

  1. Open letshang.cool in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Share button at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open letshang.cool in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ (top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen.

6 · Games + leaderboards

Kid
11

46 games, sorted

  1. Tap the Games tab.
  2. Pick a group: 🧠 Mind Benders (high-school-level brain busters), 🕹️ Arcade, 🧩 Puzzles, ❓ Quiz or 🎉 Fun.
  3. Tap the 🏆 in any game, or Leaderboards at the top, to see how you rank against your approved friends — game by game, or the all-in-one board.

Leaderboards only include your kid and their approved friends. No ads, no purchases.

12

Play a friend

  1. Under ⚔️ Play a friend, tap Sea Battle or Word Duel, then pick a friend.
  2. Your friend gets a notification and taps ✅ Let's play.
  3. Take turns — the Games tab shows a red badge when it's your move.

Sea Battle: tap a square in Enemy waters to fire. 💥 = hit, 💦 = miss. Sink every ship to win. Word Duel: pick a secret word for your friend, then guess the one they picked for you.

7 · Walkie-talkie + code names

Kid
13

Push to talk

  1. Open a chat with an approved friend.
  2. Hold the yellow 📻 button and talk (up to 15 seconds).
  3. Let go to send. Your friend hears a beep and it plays.

The first time, the phone asks to use the microphone — tap Allow. Parents can listen to every clip from Home → your kid → Friends & safety. Clips are deleted after 30 days.

14

Give a code name

  1. On the Friends tab, tap ⋯ next to a friend.
  2. Tap 🕵️ Give a code name, pick an idea or type your own, and tap Save.

Both friends see the code name. It goes through the bad-word filter, and parents always see real names.

8 · Future Me

Kid
15

Careers + money

  1. On Home, tap 🚀 Future Me.
  2. Start with the Career Quiz — 36 quick "would you enjoy this?" questions. Results show an interest code and careers to explore.
  3. Work through Career Path and Money Smarts lessons — a few short cards, then 3 quick questions each. Badges unlock as they go.
  4. Try the tools: Loan Lab (cars, homes, student loans), Budget Builder, Savings Goal, Money Grower, Credit Card Cost.

💬 Great dinner-table conversation: ask your kid what their interest code was, or have them show you the 15- vs. 30-year mortgage in Loan Lab.

16

Stock Market Challenge

  1. In Future Me, read the 📈 Stock Market lessons first (supply and demand, what a stock is, indexes, diversifying).
  2. Open 🏛️ Stock Challenge. Every kid starts with $10,000 of play money.
  3. Tap a company, enter how many shares, and tap Buy or Sell. Prices are real and update every minute.
  4. Check the friends leaderboard to see whose portfolio is winning.

Like the real market, trading only works weekdays 8:30 AM–3:00 PM Central (closed on market holidays). It's play money — nothing real is bought or sold.

Help

I forgot my password
On the parent sign-in screen tap Forgot password? and enter your username, your recovery code and a new password.
My kid forgot their PIN
Sign in as the parent → Home → your kid → Friends & safety → Name / avatar / PIN, and set a new one.
The friend request isn't showing up
Check the code was typed exactly (like HANG-M48X). After the kids accept, both parents still need to approve under Approvals.
I don't hear alert sounds
Turn the ringer switch on, turn the volume up, and tap the screen once after opening Let's Hang. Use the Test buttons under Sounds to check. Sounds only play while the app is open — turn on text alerts for when the phone is locked.
The walkie-talkie won't record
The phone needs microphone permission. On iPhone: Settings → Safari → Microphone → Allow (or Ask). Then reload letshang.cool and hold the 📻 button again.
The Stock Challenge says the market is closed
Real U.S. stock markets are open weekdays 8:30 AM–3:00 PM Central, except holidays. Kids can look at their portfolio any time but can only trade while the market is open.
The page looks old after an update
Close the tab completely and open letshang.cool again.
